# Textures

`grain.jpg` is made by `scripts/textures/pack.py` from three materials of ambientCG, each
under CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution asked; <https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/>),
taken at 2K on 2026-10-15 (at 1K the day before). Each one's height map (`_Displacement`) is
kept, its slow changes taken out, at 1024 square.

| Channel | Material | Address |

| green | Concrete036 (old concrete) | <https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete036> |

`wear.jpg` is drawn from noise by `scripts/textures/paint.py` (2026-10-15) and takes nothing
from anywhere: clay lumps in red (read by nothing since 2026-10-16), streaks of grime in green,
patches of dirt in blue, at 1024 square.
