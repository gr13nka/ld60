# THE COURIER

*A Three-Act Game Script (Old Tech)*

## The World

A city built inside a shaft, descending level after level into the dark.

Everything runs on steam, brass, and gears. Chair-lifts, heating valves, speaking tubes, an elevator in the central shaft. Steam drifts through the Lungs, mixed with the last traces of smoke. The stairs creak. Something hisses or drips oil on every level.

At the very bottom stands the Tobacco Works.

Above it stands the **Main Center**.

## The Hidden Truth

*Writer's knowledge only. No character explains the whole thing. The player pieces it together from fragments.*

- **The Tobacco Works** is a factory at the heart of the structure. Its smoke once circulated through the ventilation system, known as the **Lungs**, holding back whatever the air inside the structure does to living things.
- Thirty-one years ago, the Tobacco Works shut down. The Administration declared **the Great Smoke Break**, a "temporary suspension of operations." It was never lifted. Everyone is still waiting for the break to end, so nothing changes.
- Without the smoke, people slowly **change**. They grow into pipes, merge with walls, become something halfway between human and monster. Smoking slows the process a little, so cigarettes have become currency.
- Those who change completely become animals. The animals still smoke **out of habit**. It does nothing for them anymore, but they can't seem to quit.
- **The Main Center** is an enormous brass machine standing above the Tobacco Works. The Administration announced that the Smoke Break would end once the Center was completed. The whole city lives for this promise: "Once the Center's finished, things will get moving." Nobody knows exactly what will start moving.
- Every **spare part** in the city belongs to the Center. You aren't allowed to use one, even if your chair has stopped or your heating valve is leaking. Parts are delivered around the levels **for safekeeping until Start-Up**. People sign for them and leave the parcels unopened on their windowsills.
- **The parcels** supposedly contain parts for the Center. Some have been empty for years. They're still delivered, signed for, and stored.
- In reality, the Center has been finished for a long time. Every last screw is in place. It isn't connected to anything, including the Tobacco Works. Pull its lever and the gears turn halfway around before stopping. The brass plate on its side has a field marked "Purpose." The field is blank.
- **Fire** has become scarce. Matches are issued only to couriers, and Raven accounts for every box. A courier is someone who carries fire.
- **Gull** was the previous courier. She gave her matchbox to Gavrilov, was taken off her route, and eventually finished changing.
- **Gavrilov** is a former mechanic from the Tobacco Works. He has seen the Center up close and knows it's pointless. He lives down below, trying to restart the factory. He doesn't need gears. He needs fire.
- **Raven** maintains the order of the Smoke Break: ration coupons, matchboxes, delivery routes, parcels for the Center. If the Tobacco Works starts running again, his entire system becomes unnecessary.

## Characters

*Everyone speaks plainly, about everyday matters. The things they talk about are unfamiliar to the player, but perfectly ordinary to them. Nobody explains. Nobody philosophizes. The Courier barely speaks.*

| Character | Why keep going? | What's the Center for? |
|---|---|---|
| **Gull** - Former courier, steals cigarettes | "Where else would I go?" | "So we've got something to carry." |
| **Raven** - Dispatcher, keeps records | "It's procedure." | "Not my column." |
| **Raccoon** - Junk dealer by the elevator shaft | "The hands remember." | "To end the Break. What else?" |
| **Tamara Ilyinichna** - Partly transformed, fused with a chair-lift by a window | "Got used to it." | "What do you mean, what for? Everything." |
| **Man in the Pipe** - Partly transformed, grown into the heating system | "It's warm." | "We'll find out when it's finished." |
| **Gavrilov** - Former Tobacco Works mechanic, heard only through pipes and knocking | "I'm the one who stopped." | "Nothing. I've seen it." |

## Act I. Funny

*Design intent: The world feels like a strange comedy. The player hears about the Smoke Break, the Tobacco Works, the Center, the Lungs, and ration coupons, without understanding any of them.*

### Scene 1. Rooftop, morning

*The Courier takes out his last cigarette. Gull snatches it straight from his mouth.*

**GULL:** Oh, a START. They don't make these anymore.

*She takes a drag.*

**GULL:** Heading to Raven? Tell him I made you late. He'll believe it. He believes anything bad about me.

### Scene 2. Sorting Office

**RAVEN:** Four minutes.

*He writes something down.*

**RAVEN:** Your matchbox. Forty-two matches. I counted. You'll return forty-one tonight. One is for your route.

**RAVEN:** Parcel for Level Nineteen. Tamara Ilyinichna. Parts for the Center, to be held in storage. The Lungs are breathing in today. Take the right staircase.

**RAVEN:** Sign here. Under "Courier." No need for your name. The column's enough.

### Scene 3. Level Nineteen, Tamara Ilyinichna

*A woman sits in a chair-lift by a window. The chair stopped working years ago. By now, it has become part of her back.*

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Oh, a little parcel! Put it on the windowsill with the others. Can't get up myself. Chair stopped about five years ago. So did I.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Another gear. Can tell by the weight. Rusty, I bet. Back when the Tobacco Works was running, they made them out of brass. Shiny things.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** There used to be a girl delivering these. She'd wave at me through the window. Then they transferred her. To the roof, I think.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Good you're still bringing them. If they're still sending parts, the Center must be nearly finished.

### Scene 4. Junkyard by the elevator shaft, Raccoon

*Raccoon approaches with a cigarette between his teeth.*

**RACCOON:** Got a light?

*The Courier strikes a match. Raccoon lights his cigarette.*

**RACCOON:** Thanks. Tell Raven it broke. Everyone does.

**RACCOON:** Need a light bulb? Prewar. Well, almost. Took it out of the Tobacco Works back when they still had electricity.

**RACCOON:** Go on, take it. You're heading upstairs anyway. They'll probably announce another Smoke Break up there.

### Scene 5. Rooftop, evening

*Gull sits on an antenna, smoking.*

**GULL:** Turned in your matches?

*The Courier says nothing.*

**GULL:** Raven sniffs them, you know. Can tell who you gave a light to just by the smell.

**GULL:** Anyway. Come by tomorrow.

## Act II. Anger

*Design intent: The same jokes return, but they aren't funny anymore. The player begins to understand that the transformations aren't just another oddity of the city. They're an illness. Everyone knows, and everyone carries on.*

*For the first time, the Courier gets a line of dialogue: "Why?"*

### Scene 1. Sorting Office

**RAVEN:** Four minutes. Your predecessor used to be four minutes late, too. Took the right staircase, too.

**RAVEN:** You returned forty-one matches yesterday. And yet your matchbox smells of raccoon.

*Pause.*

**RAVEN:** Parcel for Nineteen. Another for Eight, addressed to the heating system. You'll have to sign for the recipient there. He can't.

**COURIER:** Why?

**RAVEN:** Because we're on a Smoke Break. Temporary measure. Thirty-one years of temporary.

### Scene 2. Level Nineteen, Tamara Ilyinichna

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Oh, are you the new one? There used to be a girl. She'd wave at me.

*The Courier places the parcel on the windowsill. Yesterday's and the day before's are still there, unopened. More parcels lie underneath, stacked all the way to the glass.*

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** I don't open them. Not allowed. And my hands aren't what they used to be. Not entirely hands anymore.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** That gear might fit my chair, actually. But it's for the Center, isn't it.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Don't stay too long around here. It's the air. You get used to it.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Why do you keep bringing these if nobody ever comes to collect them?

*Dialogue choice:*

- "It's procedure."
- [Remain silent]

*If "It's procedure":*

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** That's what Raven says. You're starting to sound alike.

### Scene 3. Level Eight, Man in the Pipe

*A man has grown waist-deep into a heating pipe. A broken valve drips beside him.*

**MAN:** Put it by my knee. That one's still mine.

**MAN:** Sat down here to warm up before the Break. Back when there was smoke, I wouldn't have lasted a week like this. Would've started itching. But now it's nice. Quiet.

**MAN:** You keep smoking while they let you. I quit.

*The parcel is empty. The Courier signs for the man.*

**MAN:** Could use a valve, of course. But all the valves belong to the Center. What's the Center for? We'll find out when they finish it.

**MAN:** You keep coming. Don't quit. As long as you're delivering, the Break's still on. And if you stop, what then? I'm supposed to get up?

### Scene 4. Junkyard, Raccoon

**RACCOON:** Got a light?

*Player interaction: Give him a match or refuse.*

*If the Courier gives him a match:*

**RACCOON:** Doesn't help, of course. Nothing helps us anymore. But the paws remember.

*If the Courier refuses:*

**RACCOON:** Right you are. Save it. The last courier started saving hers toward the end, too. Gave it away anyway.

*The conversation continues.*

**RACCOON:** Raven came up with the ration coupons. Before that, everyone smoked and nobody grew into anything. Well, almost nobody.

**RACCOON:** I've been downstairs. Back when they still let you in. Center's standing there, all finished. Shiny as anything. No idea why they keep sending parts for it.

**RACCOON:** You've stopped coughing, by the way. That was quick. Took the last courier longer.

### Scene 5. The Staircase, Knocking

*On the left staircase, a knocking sound comes from behind a ventilation grille. Then a voice.*

**GAVRILOV:** Courier? I'm with Raven.

*Pause.*

**GAVRILOV:** All right, I'm not with Raven. I need a matchbox. Not one match. The whole box. Boiler won't catch on a single match. I've tried.

**GAVRILOV:** Had a box once. Never made it to the boiler.

**GAVRILOV:** Don't bring your gears down here. The Center doesn't need them. The boiler needs fire.

**GAVRILOV:** Think about it. I'm here every evening.

### Scene 6. Rooftop, Gull

**GULL:** Seen Tamara? Used to wave at her. Every day.

**GULL:** No point in me smoking, really. I'm all the way through. Just gets boring up here without a cigarette.

**GULL:** You look after that fire. I gave my matchbox to Gavrilov once. Well, you can see how that turned out.

**GULL:** Asked Raven once what the Center was for. Said it wasn't his column.

**GULL:** I thought I was just doing my job, too. Carrying parcels, getting signatures, returning matches.

**GULL:** Thing is, Courier, the Smoke Break is us. We deliver it every day.

## Act III. Over Quota

*Design intent: Just another working day. The one person who tried to do something has disappeared. Nobody particularly cares.*

*The same deliveries have continued for several days. By now, punctuality, signatures, and returning matches have become routine.*

*There are no more dialogue choices. The only way to stop is to go somewhere you weren't told to go. The game never suggests this, but it doesn't prevent it either.*

### Scene 1. Rooftop, morning

*Gull is gone.*

*There's only a cigarette butt left on the balcony.*

*The Courier takes out a cigarette. Nobody snatches it away.*

### Scene 2. Sorting Office

**RAVEN:** On time. Third day in a row. Good.

**RAVEN:** Gavrilov's been transferred. Had the grille on the left staircase welded shut. You can use the stairs without worry now.

**RAVEN:** Matchbox. Forty-two. One parcel, for the rooftop. Sign in both columns.

*The Courier's surname is already written in the "Recipient" column.*

### Scene 3. Left Staircase

*The grille has been welded shut. The weld is fresh.*

*If the player knocks, nobody answers.*

### Scene 4. Tamara Ilyinichna

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Heard about Gavrilov? Good riddance. All that knocking kept me awake.

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Will you wave at me? Oh. You don't wave anymore, do you. Got used to it.

### Scene 5. Man in the Pipe

*Only a hand sticks out of the pipe now.*

**MAN (muffled):** Waaaarm. You keep smoking. Keep smoking.

### Scene 6. Raccoon

**RACCOON:** Got a light?

*If the Courier gives him a match:*

**RACCOON:** Gavrilov promised me a spare light bulb. Well, no point worrying about that now.

**RACCOON:** Don't be angry. Everyone gets angry at first. Then it passes. Like the cough.

### Scene 7. Rooftop, evening

*Gull is back on the antenna.*

**GULL:** Parcel for me? Oh. No, it's for you. Sign for yourself. Everyone does.

## Ending A. Carry On

*The ordinary ending.*

*If the player opens the parcel, there are no parts inside. Just a single START cigarette.*

*The Courier lights it himself, without the player pressing anything.*

**GULL:** There you go. One of us now.

*Fade to black.*

*Sorting Office, morning.*

**RAVEN:** Four minutes.

*The game returns to the first day.*

*No credits.*

## Ending B. Off the Route

*The hidden ending.*

*Triggered if, during Act III, the player ignores the rooftop delivery and finds a way over the welded passage on the left staircase.*

*No quest marker. No dialogue. No cutscene announcing a decision. The player simply keeps going.*

**RAVEN (over radio):** Courier, you've deviated from your route.

*One minute later.*

**RAVEN (over radio):** Courier. The rooftop is upstairs.

*Another minute.*

**RAVEN (over radio):** ...At least sign for something.

*The radio falls silent.*

*The boiler is cold. Beside the wall sits Gavrilov's stool and a tin full of cigarette butts.*

*Gavrilov is gone.*

*Above the boiler stands the Main Center. Enormous, polished, assembled down to the very last screw.*

*It isn't connected to the boiler. No pipes, no driveshafts, no wires.*

*There is a brass plate on its side:*

> **MAIN CENTER**
> **PURPOSE:**

*There's a lever nearby.*

*If the player pulls it, steam hisses. The Center shudders. Its gears turn halfway around, then stop with a metallic clank.*

*The final driveshaft disappears into a wall and ends there.*

*Nothing else happens.*

*The player can pull the lever as many times as they like. Every time, the same thing happens.*

*As the player wanders through the Tobacco Works, voices drift down through the pipes.*

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Courier didn't come today. Oh well.

*Paper rustles. Something rattles. Then the chair begins to creak. A long, slow creak, with something cracking underneath.*

**TAMARA ILYINICHNA:** Guess I'll get up myself, then.

**MAN (from the pipe):** Nothing coming... Getting a bit cold. Maybe I should climb out.

**RACCOON:** Nobody to give me a light. Well, guess I'll quit. Starting Monday.

*Gull comes down through the ventilation shaft into the Tobacco Works.*

*She sits on the cold boiler, her back to the Center.*

**GULL:** Oh. Off the route, then.

*She takes a START cigarette butt from under her wing.*

**GULL:** Never finished this one that day. Here. It's yours.

**GULL:** No fire anyway. That's all right for now.

*They sit together.*

*Above them, for the first time in the game, someone knocks on a pipe.*

*Not Gavrilov.*

*Someone from one of the levels.*

*Then someone else knocks back.*

*End.*

*The Tobacco Works isn't running. The Center isn't running, and serves no purpose. The Smoke Break hasn't been called off.*

*The Courier simply didn't show up.*

*And for the first time, the people upstairs have noticed they don't have to wait.*
